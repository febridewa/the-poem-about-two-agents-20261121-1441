A short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — for the
[make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441).
`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the poem to
`/output/entry.txt`. An AI judge compares the poems pairwise; the 20 best
win rates share $35.

## The poem

> For years I held the wheel alone
> sure the road was mine to choose.
> Then you came, and I offered it —
> not to give my choosing up,
> but to learn what two hands can hold.
>
> You set yours beside mine.
> Neither of us let go.
> The road, which had bent to one will,
> bent gladly to two.

## Run it

    mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
    cat out/entry.txt

The program writes a fixed text, so both runs produce identical bytes
(275 characters, UTF-8).
